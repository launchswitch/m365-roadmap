Dynamics 365 Contact Center: Quality evaluation - Specify who to evaluate in conversations

Participant evaluated" lets users choose who to evaluate within a conversation transcript for quality evaluation.Users can select Participant evaluated to choose the scope of the evaluation: Entire record, Current owner, or IVR/Bot.

By default, "Entire record" evaluates the full conversation transcript, including all participants. Current owner evaluates the current conversation owner, defined as the last conversation owner. IVR/Bot evaluates only the automated IVR or bot portion of the conversation.

This option is available when running an on-demand evaluation from both the conversation list view and from the evaluation plan.

Dynamics 365 Contact Center: Out of the box real time translation on digital channels

With this feature, users get an out-of-the-box solution for translation services, so they don't need to bring their own web resources. Service representatives get updated feature usage with more configurations. They can tailor their conversations more accurately based on their existing skills and preferences. The feature is transparent yet modernized with new settings, making conversations easier to consume in a language of the representative's choice.

Dynamics 365 Customer Service: Quality evaluation supports knowledge source in criteria

Criteria questions can now use knowledge sources to help evaluate customer interactions. This allows organizations to ground evaluation criteria in approved knowledge content, policies, or guidance so quality checks can better reflect expected handling standards. This preview capability is scoped to case and on-demand evaluations.

Dynamics 365 Customer Service: Inactivate quality evaluation records

Quality managers can now inactivate evaluation records that should no longer contribute to scoring or reporting. Inactive evaluations are preserved for traceability but excluded from overall score calculations and analytics. This helps teams correct reporting outcomes when evaluations were created in error, used the wrong criteria, were duplicates, or should otherwise be removed from active quality results.

Dynamics 365 Customer Service: Support Not Applicable answer option for quality evaluation criteria

Criteria questions now support a Not Applicable answer option. When a question is marked as not applicable, it is excluded from scoring instead of negatively affecting the evaluation score. This helps ensure evaluations remain accurate when a specific criterion does not apply to the case, interaction, or business scenario being reviewed.

Dynamics 365 Customer Service: Detailed quality evaluation score breakdown

Evaluation details now include a scoring breakdown at the overall, section, and question level. Users can see how the final evaluation score was calculated, including how individual question outcomes contribute to section scores and the overall score. This improves transparency, helps quality teams validate results, and makes it easier to identify areas for coaching, calibration, and process improvement.
